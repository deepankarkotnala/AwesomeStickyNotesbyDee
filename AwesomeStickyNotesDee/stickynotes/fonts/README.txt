Fonts bundled with Awesome Sticky Notes
=======================================

These are the original, unmodified font files from Google Fonts (https://github.com/google/fonts).
The app loads them at start-up so every note can use them, whether or not they are installed on the computer.
Each font's licence is in the "licences" folder.
